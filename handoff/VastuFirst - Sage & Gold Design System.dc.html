02 · Foundations

Zone map & colourblind test

Nine hues, adjacent on a circular map, must each be identifiable at a glance while staying calm. They are a modulated family, not nine loud independents. Colour is never the only signal: every zone is also fixed by position (N always top) and a label rendered in ink — so it survives red-green colour blindness and greyscale alike.

03 · Foundations

Typography

Three voices — Marcellus serif for display, DM Sans for body, DM Mono for numerals and labels. The prototype's 13.5px body was too small for a middle-aged, outdoor audience: the floor is now 16sp body / 12sp minimum. Sizes bias larger and everything reflows to 200%.

Token Specimen Family · size/lh · wt · track

Six scripts, one product · §6

Marcellus and DM cover no Indic script — so five of six languages are paired with matched Noto Serif and Noto Sans optical sizes. Indic scripts get 1.5–1.6 line-height against Latin's 1.2, because of matras and conjuncts. No component that holds translatable text may be fixed-height.

Numerals — Latin by default
The score, degrees and prices use Western Arabic (0–9) across all locales — near-universally read in urban India and consistent in DM Mono. Devanagari numerals ship as a per-locale option in Settings, not a default. The score is a number and appears everywhere; one numeral system keeps it stable.
Reflow at 200%
Text scales to 200% system setting. Dense screens (report, settings) reflow single-column; the score number caps at a max so it never clips its container; buttons grow in height, never truncate their label. Line-boxes are set from the tallest script, so Devanagari never re-lays-out a screen proofed in English.

Numbers the implementer can test, not adjectives. The soft sage-and-ivory palette is exactly where low contrast hides — so every critical pair is measured, live, below. Rows marked Large/UI clear 3:1 and are restricted to ≥18sp text, icons or borders; they never carry body copy.

Zone hues and the gold accent are deliberate exceptions: zone-e and gold sit near or below 3:1 as fills, so they never carry meaning alone — the ink zone-letter and fixed position do.
